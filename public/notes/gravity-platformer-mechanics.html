How 4-Way Gravity Platformer Physics Works in Godot 4

In classic gravity-flip platformers like VVVVVV or Celeste mods, responsive control comes from deterministic velocity decomposition rather than physics-engine forces.

1. Tangent and Normal Velocity Split

Every physics frame, the player's world velocity is projected onto two axes: the gravity normal (falling/jumping) and the screen-relative tangent (running). When you stand on the ceiling, pressing D still moves you right on screen—preventing control inversion sickness.

2. Anti-Catapult Momentum Cap

Flipping gravity in mid-air preserves your world momentum, but caps speed directed against the new gravity vector to 240 px/s so you can weave through spike corridors cleanly.
