Rhombus Crystal (◆) Routing & Gold Medal Speedrun Guide

Every stage exit in Anti-Gravity Jump starts locked. To unlock the golden Goal and earn the ★ GOLD rank (0 deaths, under 25 seconds), you must chain your mid-air flips through each cyan Rhombus Crystal.

Level 01 — First Flip (3 ◆)

  • Crystal 1: Flip UP right after the first step to grab the ceiling rhombus, then drop back down.
  • Crystal 2: Run along the ceiling over the spike pit, flip DOWN mid-pit to touch the floating rhombus, and immediately flip UP using your restored air charge.
  • Crystal 3: Grab the lower-left chamber rhombus before flipping LEFT onto the vertical wall.
