Every Wall Is a Floor

Flip your personal gravity vector in any of the 4 cardinal directions (Down, Up, Left, Right). Run across ceilings to clear spike pits or turn vertical shafts into walkways.

Tactical Rhombus Lock (◆)

Rushing to the end isn't enough. Every stage exit starts locked with a crimson barrier. Grab every floating cyan Rhombus Crystal to unlock the Goal while refilling your mid-air flip charge.

Ghost Trajectory & Zero-Lag Audio

A live raycast Ghost Line shows your exact landing spot before you commit to a flip, backed by procedural 8-bit synthesizer audio and sub-second checkpoint respawns.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I unlock the exit door?

The exit Goal stays locked (marked with a red X) until you collect every cyan Rhombus Crystal (◆) in the room. Check the top-left HUD counter to see how many crystals remain.

How do mid-air gravity flips work?

Flipping gravity while standing on a surface is free and unlimited. In mid-air, you have 1 air-flip charge (shown as a golden dot below your gravity compass). Landing on any surface or touching a Rhombus Crystal immediately refills your charge.
